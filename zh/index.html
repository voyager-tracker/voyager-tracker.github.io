POWER SYSTEM

Each spacecraft is powered by Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs), which convert heat from decaying plutonium-238 into electricity.

Combined RTG output declines by about 4 watts per year, requiring the mission team to progressively power down instruments.

High Power→ Power Management→ Instrument Shutdown→ Extended Mission
